Abia

Abia is a State in Nigeria. It is bordered by Imo to the East, Enugu to the north, Akwa Ibom to the South and Port Harcourt to the south south. The state is a commercial city and attracts great commerce. The people here are industrious and work to make the state proud. Aba in the commercial nerve center of the state and is being touted as the Japan of Africa. In Aba you will see the Aba Nigeria Temple towering on an elevated spot and the view at night is wonderful.
